`delivery packs inspect <pack>` shows, for a pack that comes with the harness, what it is for, what each workflow does and where an idea may go, what the agent may touch, how far each workflow goes before it asks you (its guards and its sign-off, or that it lands once you approve the idea), that the project's own checks and landing are used, a fingerprint that changes when any instruction changes, and then every instruction in full, the pack's words apart from the harness's. It runs nothing, writes nothing and asks nothing.
